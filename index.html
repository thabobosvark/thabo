001 / about

The person
behind the terminal

I grew up in Kimberley - the city of diamonds - and I'm building something just as hard to crack. I started a Computer Science degree at UP, but financial barriers forced me to step away. I didn't stop. I transferred to CPUT, earned 11 distinctions, joined South Africa's national HPC team, and got selected for the ISC26 international competition in Hamburg, Germany.

I'm in my final year of Computer Engineering, studying Electronic Communications, Embedded Systems, Network Systems, Database Systems, and my Industrial Design Project. My goal is to go deep into HPC, Networking, and Cybersecurity, with an eye on cloud infrastructure and finance analytics too.

When I'm not in a terminal, I'm on the tennis court or sketching cluster diagrams for fun. I speak English, Tswana, Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa, Sotho, and Sepedi. Still learning - always.
