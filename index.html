The person
behind the work

I grew up in Kimberley, the city of diamonds, and I'm building something just as hard to earn. I started a Computer Science degree at the University of Pretoria, but financial barriers forced me to step away. I didn't stop.

I transferred to CPUT, earned 12 distinctions out of 14 modules, joined South Africa's national HPC team, and got selected for ISC26 in Hamburg, Germany.

I'm in my final year of Computer Engineering, working through Electronic Communications, Embedded Systems, Network Systems, Database Systems, and my Industrial Design Project. My focus is HPC, networking, and cybersecurity, with an eye on cloud infrastructure and finance analytics too.

When I'm not in a terminal, I'm on the tennis court or sketching cluster diagrams for fun.

Languages: English, Tswana, Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa, Sotho, and Sepedi. Still learning, always.
